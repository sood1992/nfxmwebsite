Creative Strategy

Every project starts with strategy—not guesswork.
We audit your competitors, decode what your audience actually wants,
and build a creative playbook tied to clear business goals.
Reels aren’t random. Campaigns aren’t trendy.
Everything is rooted in insight, psychology, and platform science.
This is how we ship content that doesn’t just look good—it performs.

Growth Strategy

We don’t post and pray—or boost random ads hoping something sticks.
We build creative-led, performance-driven systems that grow your brand with intent.
From planned social media grids and monthly content calendars to paid ads, lead magnets,
email flows, and retargeting funnels. —everything is stitched into a strategy built to convert.
We reverse-engineer attention, test fast, and scale what prints.
Measured in: CPL, CTR, CAC, ROAS—not fire emojis and fluff.
If it doesn’t sell, it doesn’t ship. If it scales, we pour fuel.

Creative Production

We don’t just make things look pretty—we make them work hard.
Every film, ad, and reel is shot with a clear goal: attention → emotion → action.
We deliver export-ready assets across formats (reels, YouTube, Meta, TV, web),
with conversion-focused framing, CTA-aligned scripting, and editing that’s built for platform behaviour.
Measured in: Watchtime, CTR, Save Rate, Retargeting Potential.
If it doesn't hold attention, it doesn't ship.

AI & Automation

This is where human creativity meets machine speed
—and where your workflows stop dragging their feet.
We build GPT-powered content agents, automate edits, route files with n8n,
and track wins live through smart dashboards.
Yes, we make AI agents that replace entire roles—from editors to copywriters to project managers.
Your team gets to focus on what matters. The bots? They never ask for chai breaks.
Measured in: Hours saved, tasks automated, output increased.
Creative output goes up. Burnout goes down. Neofox just gave you a second team.

Web & UX

Your website should feel like your best salesperson—fast, confident, and frictionless.
We build pages that load in under 3 seconds, guide users smoothly to CTA, and log every click.
UX audits are backed by heatmaps, session recordings, and funnel data.
Measured in: Load speed, bounce rate, conversion rate, time on page.
If people land and leave, you’re leaking revenue. We plug it.
