About project

Bajaj Auto has always been more than a brand—it’s a symbol of Indian engineering and grit. Neofox partnered with Bajaj to tell that story with the same clarity and power their rides deliver on the road. How do you evolve a legacy brand while staying true to its roots?Bajaj needed campaigns that spoke to loyal riders and a new generation—highlighting innovation, engineering excellence, and a commitment to sustainability.How do you evolve a legacy brand while staying true to its roots?

This wasn’t just a shoot — it was an expedition. Through mountain passes, remote villages, and insane weather, we captured what the Dominar was built for. Every frame? Real riders, real roads, real grit.
This film had one goal — to make the first ride feel unforgettable. From casting to camera work, we leaned into that “new bike energy” and shot it like a love letter to city riders. Clean. Confident. Classic Pulsar.
From launch pads to Ladakh trails, we didn’t just make content—we built mileage. Bajaj brought the machines. We brought the story.
What began as a workshop film became a tribute to Bajaj’s makers — the hands, minds, and moments behind every machine. Shot amidst the rhythm of real work, it’s our way of showing how precision can feel deeply human.
